THE ATELIER MANIFESTO

We do not simply cook.
We choreograph the quiet memory of soil, fire, & season.

Founded in 2018 along the untamed ridges of the Northern Valley, AURA emerged from a deep conviction: fine dining had grown detached from the living earth. We dismantled the white tablecloths, silenced the sterile formulas, and ignited a white birch hearth.

CHAPTER I • FOUNDATION

The Wild Highland Forage

Chef Julian Vance and botanical researcher Hélène Mercier spent eight months living in the alpine ridges, cataloging 140 indigenous fungal networks, wild herbs, and mountain birch saps. The first menus were penned entirely by firelight.

“Before we struck our first match in the kitchen, we promised the mountain we would never take more than the forest was willing to give.”
OUR COVENANT

Four Principles We Never Break

In an era of artificial speed and simulated flavors, these four non-negotiable convictions anchor every breath taken inside our atelier.

PRINCIPIUM 01

Hyper-Local Terroir

Zero food miles where possible. Over 98% of our botanicals, heirloom grains, and river catches originate within 60 miles of our front hearth.

PRINCIPIUM 02

Living Birch Embers

We reject magnetic induction. Our cooking relies on white-hot white birch and seasoned oak coals, honoring the primal cadence of fire and ember.

PRINCIPIUM 03

Producer Reverence

We deal directly with 24 multi-generational agrarian families and free divers, paying 40% above fair-market rates to protect regenerative farming.

PRINCIPIUM 04

The Circular Hearth

Zero waste in practice. Spent vegetable tops become wild vinegars, animal bones ferment into rich garums, and clean wood ash clarifies our broths.
